# 💰 TrustLedger

### AI-Powered Digital Udhaar & Trust Management Platform

> **Turn informal Udhaar into intelligent, transparent, and actionable financial records.**

TrustLedger is an **AI-powered digital Udhaar management platform** designed especially for **small vendors, local businesses, and underserved users** who often depend on handwritten notebooks and informal credit systems.

Instead of asking *“Who owes me?”* or searching through pages of records, TrustLedger turns everyday transactions into a **clear digital financial picture** — helping users track outstanding balances, repayments, receipts, collections, and trustworthiness in one place.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Millions of small vendors and local businesses still manage credit transactions using:

* 📒 Handwritten Udhaar notebooks
* 🧠 Memory-based payment tracking
* 🧾 Unorganized receipts
* ❓ Uncertainty about who owes what
* ⏰ Missed or overdue repayments
* 📉 No structured record of repayment behavior

This makes everyday credit management **time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to scale**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**TrustLedger digitizes the complete Udhaar journey.**

A vendor can record a transaction and use TrustLedger to:

**Record → Track → Analyze → Collect → Build Trust**

The platform transforms raw transaction information into useful insights such as:

> **“Who owes me the most?”**

> **“How much money am I owed?”**

> **“Which payments are overdue?”**

> **“Who do I owe the most?”**

Instead of simply storing transactions, TrustLedger helps users **understand their money.**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎙️ Voice-Friendly Transaction Entry

Record transactions using simple voice-based input, making digital financial management more accessible to users with limited digital literacy.

### 💳 Digital Udhaar Management

Replace scattered notebooks with organized digital records of credit transactions.

### 📊 Outstanding Balance Tracking

Know exactly how much is receivable or payable and identify the people with the highest outstanding balances.

### 🔔 Repayment & Collection Tracking

Track repayments and identify pending or overdue amounts so users can prioritize collections.

### 🧾 Digital Receipts

Generate and maintain transaction receipts for clearer records and better transparency.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Financial Insights

Ask natural-language questions about your transaction data and receive actionable answers instead of manually searching through records.

### ⭐ Trust Score

Analyze repayment behavior to create a simple trust indicator that helps users understand financial reliability.

### 📱 Simple & Accessible Interface

Designed with small vendors and everyday users in mind — focusing on clarity rather than complicated financial terminology.
